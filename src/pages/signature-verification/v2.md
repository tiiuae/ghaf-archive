# Signature verification instructions

## Ghaf 24.09 - 24.09.2

---

A note about older releases: These Ghaf releases are deprecated, and their Azure-based signature verification service has been retired.

Please use Ghaf 25.12.1 or newer, signed with NetHSM, and follow the verification instructions for your chosen release. Thank you for using Ghaf!
